Thought I'd write down the flow of thoughts that went through my head as I started building this project.

I think the thing that I wanted the most at the beginning was something that stood out; something that oozed personality. But the problem with that is that it is difficult to make work and might be easily misinterpreted, as opposed to just a frankly boilerplate site. But then I stumbled upon some portfolio sites for some REALLY cool frontend devs, and I thought, hey, I do want something like that. 

So I started putting down code.

But try as I might, it just all felt so... dry? Empty? Uninspired? 

I kept teeter-tottering on  what I wanted, and in the end, just made a navbar (which I revised like a billion times and only for desktop) because I literally couldn't come up with anything worthwhile.

So the viscious cycle started again. Look for ideas, find a dime-a-dozen dev site, think "hey, I want that!", put down 5 lines of code, lose interest because it felt dry, try to find "my style", give up, rinse, repeat. 

No. I'm gonna sit down, and start properly working on something. 

So, what's a site that looks neat? [Yuta Abe's](https://yuta-abe.com/) website looks FRIGGIN COOL man. Let's try for something to that effect then, in react, because people would like the sound of that on a resume. Let's give it my best shot. Oh, and add some API stuff, because that's my strong suit I think!
